UNDERSTAND THE TRADEOFFS

Debt relief.
With eyes open.

Debt settlement is one possible approach, not a guaranteed solution. Compare alternatives before choosing a provider.

KNOW THE RISKS

Settlement has consequences.

Debt settlement can damage credit. Missed payments can increase interest and fees and trigger collection activity or lawsuits. Creditors may decline to settle; forgiven debt may have tax consequences.

COMPARE YOUR OPTIONS

There’s more than one path.

Consider speaking directly with creditors about hardship options, consulting a nonprofit credit counselor, or discussing legal options with a qualified attorney. This site does not recommend stopping payments.

ASK BEFORE AGREEING

Understand every term.

Ask a provider about total fees, realistic timing, required savings, risks, and state availability. For debt relief services covered by the FTC’s Telemarketing Sales Rule, advance fees are prohibited until its fee conditions are met.

Independent resources: CFPB: debt relief risks and alternatives · FTC: debt relief rules. Links are for education and do not imply government endorsement.

03 / Clear answers

Glad you
asked.

A few things to know before you ring the bell. Read the risks, privacy notice, and disclosures before reaching out.

What exactly do you do?

Saved By The Bell Financial is a marketing and referral company. We help interested consumers explore debt-relief services through independent providers. We are not a lender, law firm, credit counselor, or debt settlement provider, and do not negotiate with creditors.

How does enrollment work?

Start by requesting information through this website. A service provider must review your circumstances, explain its fees and risks, confirm state availability, and determine eligibility before you choose whether to enroll. Submitting this form does not itself enroll you.

Does it cost anything to reach out?

There is no cost to submit an inquiry to us. A provider may charge for its services. Review all fees, payment terms, and cancellation rights before agreeing to a program.

Will I qualify? How long does it take?

Eligibility and timing depend on your state, financial situation, debts, and the provider’s requirements. Approval, savings, timelines, creditor participation, and results are not guaranteed.

What happens when I start an inquiry?

Fill out the inquiry form with your name, email, state, and topic. It prepares an email draft for you to review and send in your email app. The website does not send the form automatically. You can also email us directly. Please don’t include account numbers, your Social Security number, or other sensitive financial details.
